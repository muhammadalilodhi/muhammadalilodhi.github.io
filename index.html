Muhammad Ali Lodhi, Ph.D.

Yangzhou, China · alilodhi30@gmail.com

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the School of Information Engineering, Yangzhou University, China, specializing in Artificial Intelligence, TinyML, the Internet of Things (IoT), and resource optimization for large-scale wireless sensor and LPWAN networks. My research focuses on developing intelligent, energy-efficient, and explainable communication frameworks for LoRaWAN, underwater networks, and next-generation resource-constrained IoT systems.

I received my Ph.D. in Software Engineering from Dalian University of Technology, where my dissertation investigated optimization techniques for LPWAN-based IoT networks. My work has been published in IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing, IEEE Internet of Things Journal, IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management, and other leading journals, with 17+ SCI-indexed publications to date. I actively collaborate on interdisciplinary projects spanning TinyML, wireless communications, reinforcement learning, federated learning, and autonomous networked systems.

Research Interests

My work centers on making resource-constrained wireless networks smarter and more efficient. This spans intelligent, explainable resource allocation for LoRaWAN and LPWAN-based IoT systems, on-device TinyML for adaptive data rate and channel selection, and reinforcement learning approaches to mobility-aware and energy-efficient network management.

I am equally interested in extending these ideas to related domains — including UAV-assisted and drone-based IoT networks, underwater optical communication, and federated learning for distributed, autonomous networked systems — and in bridging rigorous algorithm design with practical, deployable embedded solutions.
